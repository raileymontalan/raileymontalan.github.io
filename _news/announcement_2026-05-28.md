More wins for Southeast Asia: our paper on [expanding evaluations to Burmese](https://lrec.elra.info/lrec2026-main-016) was presented at LREC 2026! And last month, we released our collection of [embedding models optimized for SEA languages](https://huggingface.co/collections/aisingapore/sea-lion-modernbert-and-embedding); this [work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12243) will be presented at ACL 2026 in July.

At sa nakaraáng buwán, inilabás námin ang áming [koleksiyón ng mga embedding model na in-optimize pára sa mga wikà ng SEA](https://huggingface.co/collections/aisingapore/sea-lion-modernbert-and-embedding); ipepresentá ang [akdâng itó](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12243) sa ACL 2026 sa Húlyo._</span>
