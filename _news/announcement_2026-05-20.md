[Version 4.5 of SEA-LION](https://huggingface.co/collections/aisingapore/sea-lion-v45) was released today! These were built upon the [Qwen 3.6 27B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B) and [Gemma 4 E2B](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-E2B-it) architectures, and underwent distillation from stronger models using our SEA [instruction-tuning dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/aisingapore/SEA-Instruct-2602) to increase their multilingual and multicultural fluency. Try them out [here](https://sea-lion.ai/try-sea-lion/) today!
