[Version 5 of SEA-LION](https://huggingface.co/collections/aisingapore/sea-lion-v5) is out, our largest model family yet! It spans five models built on Qwen, Nemotron and Gemma, from the compact [Gemma-SEA-LION-v5-E2B](https://huggingface.co/aisingapore/Gemma-SEA-LION-v5-E2B) to our 397B mixture-of-experts flagship, [Qwen-SEA-LION-v5-397B-A17B-Plus](https://huggingface.co/aisingapore/Qwen-SEA-LION-v5-397B-A17B-Plus), which leads the Pareto frontier among open models of similar or larger size on [SEA-HELM](https://leaderboard.sea-lion.ai). I helped evaluate these models on SEA-HELM. Try [them](https://sea-lion.ai/try-sea-lion/) out today!
